Privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026. NilObs is SIRE 2.0 inspection prep at nilobs.com.

In short: you sign in with Google to use NilObs. We keep your name, email and photo link, plus your NilObs progress, so it follows you to every device. We don't sell anything about you, show no ads and use no tracking cookies. You can delete your account at any time.

On the public site

The game and readiness check on the home page run in your browser. Their results stay in your browser (local storage) and nothing about you is sent to us. Opening the app itself needs a Google sign-in.

If you sign in with Google

  • From Google: your Google account ID, name, email address and the link to your profile photo. Nothing else: we don't ask for access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive or anything in your Google account.
  • Your profile: your role, ship type and, only if you add it, your company's name.
  • Your NilObs data: XP, streak and badges, flashcard schedule, interview scores, inspection briefs, ratings, Ask NilObs chat history and close-out drafts, so they sync between your devices.
  • Your wallet: your credits, plan and their history (what was spent on what, and when), kept on our server so they can't be lost or changed on a device.
  • A sign-in cookie (nilobs_sid) that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. We store only a scrambled (hashed) copy of it.

We use Google's information only to sign you in and show who is signed in. Our use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see your account

The NilObs team can see account details in a private admin dashboard, to run, support and improve the service: your name, email, sign-up and last-visit dates, role, ship type, company if you gave one, XP and streak, credits and plan, what credits were spent on (for example "Ask NilObs" or "Photo check") and payments. The dashboard does not show what you typed, asked or photographed.

The AI features

  • Questions, answers you type or speak, and photos you send to Photo check go to Anthropic (Claude) to produce the reply. Photos are shrunk on your phone first, which removes location data, and NilObs doesn't store them; a small thumbnail stays in your browser only.
  • When the AI inspector speaks, the question's text goes to Rumik to make the audio.
  • Please keep names of people, ships and companies out of what you type and photograph.

Where it lives

The site and your account data run on Cloudflare (Workers and D1). Like any website, Cloudflare handles your IP address to deliver pages; we use it only to limit how often the AI can be called.

Payments

Payments are handled by Cashfree Payments. You enter your card, UPI or bank details on Cashfree's checkout, never ours, and we never see or store them. To start a payment we send Cashfree your name, email address and the mobile number you type in (Cashfree requires one); your mobile number is also remembered in your browser for next time. Cashfree tells us whether the payment went through. We keep a record of each payment (what was bought, the amount, the date and Cashfree's payment reference) for our accounts and to handle refunds. Cashfree keeps its own records under its privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Signed in, open Profile & progress → Delete account (or tap your photo at the top, then Your profile). Your account, sign-in sessions, saved progress and wallet are erased from our database straight away. We keep two things: the payment records above (with no name or email attached), and a one-way scrambled code made from your Google account ID, so that signing up again doesn't give out the free starting credits twice. The code can't be turned back into your ID or email. Signing out instead keeps your account and clears this device. You can also email us to ask for a copy or deletion.
