Training & certificates: what SIRE 2.0 inspectors ask and find

The officers' matrix, discharge books, endorsements and course certificates have to match each other and the ship type.

What the inspector wants to see

  • Officers' matrix matching discharge books
  • Cargo endorsements for the ship type
  • Simulator and BRM/ERM course certificates in date

Questions inspectors ask about Training & certificates

Can all familiarisation items, including reading the SMS manual, be signed on the same day?
No. Spread them over the recommended periods (e.g. within 24 hrs, 7 days and 30 days). Everything signed on one date was flagged.
Which familiarisation items apply specifically to the ETO?
Power generation, electrical and electronic equipment, and all bridge alarms. Inspectors noted there was no dedicated ETO page in the checklist.
Which familiarisation items are often missed for catering staff and engineers?
Work familiarisation for catering staff, CO2/foam release for everyone, and cargo watch items for engineers (CCR alarms, BWTS start/stop, IG/N2, power pack, VECS).
What do inspectors expect for behavioural competency assessments (BCA)?
A BCA summary per crew member since joining, showing progress in navigation, cargo, mooring, engineering and bunkering, done by designated assessors with valid training.
What engine room simulator training must engineers have?
A shore-based ER simulator course within 5 years, per IMO model 1.39/2.07: 21 hrs (3 days) operational level, 40 hrs (5 days) management level. A one-day course was flagged.
What should an ER simulator course certificate state?
Emergency machinery operation, the ship's propulsion type and the IMO model course duration. Certificates missing these were flagged.
