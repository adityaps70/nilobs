PIQ accuracy: what SIRE 2.0 inspectors ask and find

The pre-inspection questionnaire has to match the ship as she is on the day, including photos, so every change on board needs to reach the PIQ.

Do this before the inspector arrives: Compare PIQ photos and data with the ship as she is today.

What the inspector wants to see

  • PIQ photos and data updated after any change
  • Photos date-stamped, taken with the intrinsically safe camera

Questions inspectors ask about PIQ accuracy

What makes an acceptable photo for the SIRE 2.0 photo upload?
It must show the true current condition, be representative of the area and free of shadows. Use close deck views, show access in the steering gear room view, remove the ETA canvas cover.
Why must the HVPQ be checked thoroughly before an inspection?
The compiled VIQ (CVIQ) is generated largely from HVPQ data, so any HVPQ error produces wrong questions in the inspection.
From when does the Estimated Index Value (EIV) apply, as asked in HVPQ 1.2.4?
From 1 January 2023, for vessels that do not have an EEXI.
What mooring drum details go into the HVPQ?
Details of every mooring drum. The SBM mooring line storage drum has a larger diameter than the others.
Can you upload a photo taken on a sister ship?
No. A photo from another vessel was flagged because it did not show the double block and bleed valve fitted on board.
If HVPQ 5.2.1.1 (helicopter operations) is answered 'yes', what must be in place?
Helicopter RA, training/drill requirements, landing area plan, ICS helicopter checklist, assigned officer and crew, emergency equipment, limits on cargo/crane work.
