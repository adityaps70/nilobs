GMDSS: what SIRE 2.0 inspectors ask and find

GMDSS findings usually come from the logbook: daily, weekly and monthly tests, battery checks and EPIRB and SART tests that match the equipment.

Do this before the inspector arrives: Do the GMDSS battery on/off-load test and update the log.

What the inspector wants to see

  • GMDSS log with daily, weekly and monthly tests
  • Battery on-load and off-load test records
  • EPIRB and SART test records

Questions inspectors ask about GMDSS

How often is VDR data downloaded?
Quarterly, per the company in the notes.
Can the LRIT Sat-C and SSAS Sat-C be used interchangeably?
No.
How is Sea Area A3 now defined?
An area outside A1 and A2 within coverage of a recognised mobile satellite service (Inmarsat or Iridium) supported by the ship's SES, with continuous alerting.
Is NBDP still part of GMDSS equipment?
No. NBDP is now redundant and no longer part of GMDSS equipment.
What rules apply to portable two-way radios?
Keep them on trickle charge at all times, do not test on Ch 16, and know the limits on using non-IS radios outside the bridge.
What is the difference between a distress alert and a distress message?
Hold the distress button about 4 s to send the alert; holding longer may bring up a list to choose the nature of distress.
