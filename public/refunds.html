Refunds and cancellations

Last updated 20 October 2026.

In short: a failed AI answer is refunded in credits automatically. If you were charged and didn't get what you paid for, or were charged twice, we refund you in full. If you change your mind within 7 days and haven't used what you bought, we refund that too.

Failed AI answers

If an AI answer, photo check or grade fails or never arrives, the credits it cost are returned to your wallet automatically. A photo that isn't of a ship, its equipment or a shipboard record is refunded the same way.

Payment problems

If you paid but the credits or plan didn't arrive, or you were charged twice, email us with the date and amount. We'll add what you paid for, or refund the payment in full, usually within 2 working days.

Changing your mind

  • Top-ups and the Voyage pass: within 7 days of buying, if you haven't spent any of the credits it gave you, we'll refund it in full.
  • Plans: within 7 days of buying, if you've used fewer than a quarter of that plan's first month's credits, we'll refund it in full and move you back to Free.
  • After that, purchases aren't refundable, but paid credits stay in your wallet as described in the terms.

Cancelling

Plans are paid up front and don't renew by themselves, so there is no subscription to cancel and you'll never be charged again unless you choose to buy more. When a plan ends you move to the Free plan and keep your XP, streak and saved work.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the way you paid, through Razorpay (rupees) or Stripe (dollars). Once we've issued one, banks usually take 5 to 10 working days to show it. Credits from a refunded purchase are removed from your wallet.

Deleting your account

Deleting your account erases its credits and plan. If you have paid credits left that you'd like refunded under this policy, email us before you delete it.

Asking for a refund

Email adityaps700@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, saying what you bought and when.
